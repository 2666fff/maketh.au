STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Searching for the cheapest website design?

Is Maketh the cheapest website designer in Australia?

We do not claim to be the cheapest provider in Australia. Our cheapest published package is AUD $199. Compare the same page count, domain term, hosting term, mobile layout, source ownership and renewal costs when comparing quotes.

Are these packages available in Sydney, Melbourne and Perth?

Yes. DanaTech / Maketh is based in Mount Gravatt, Brisbane and accepts remote projects across Australia, including Sydney, Melbourne, Perth and WA. Brisbane meetings are available by appointment; other locations are served remotely.

Does every website work on mobile and desktop?

Yes. Desktop and mobile adaptation is included in the $199, $299 and $888 website packages.

Can the $299 package email me every enquiry?

Email notifications are not part of the advertised $299 package. Submitted quotes are stored for the owner to review and manage in the private dashboard. Any additional notification integration needs a separate agreed scope.

What should I check before paying for a cheap website?

Confirm the exact pages and functions, who supplies content, domain eligibility and ownership, hosting duration, renewal charges and source-code handover. Ask for a working example and a written scope, not just a headline price.

Tell us what your website needs to do.

Send your page list, package preference and any custom functions. We confirm the scope before work starts.
