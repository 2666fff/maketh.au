STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you start

What do I need to provide?

Your logo, page text, product or service images, contact details and a few design references. We confirm the page list, functions and delivery date before starting.

What happens after the first year?

Domain and hosting renewals are quoted separately before renewal. Ongoing content updates and maintenance are optional, separately quoted services.

How do I compare cheap website quotes?

Compare the same pages and functions, desktop and mobile layouts, domain and hosting terms, source-code ownership and renewal costs. Ask for a working example and a written scope.

Tell us what your website needs to do.

Send your page list, package preference and any custom functions. We confirm the scope before work starts.
